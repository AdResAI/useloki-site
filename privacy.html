Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 12, 2026

The short version

Loki runs entirely in your browser. It does not create an account or an identifier for you, and it sends nothing anywhere: this version has no server and makes no network requests of its own. You can see everything Loki has recorded, export it, and erase it from the extension popup.

What Loki stores on your device

In Hide me mode Loki observes nothing about the pages you visit: no page is classified, no profile is updated, no site is recorded. The following applies to Monetize me. All of it lives in your browser's extension storage and is deleted when you reset the ledger or uninstall Loki. None of it is transmitted.

The ledger

One row per ad impression Loki observed in Monetize me mode: the site, the slot size and position, the bid or estimate used to price it, where that number came from, its confidence, and whether the ad was in view or clicked. Rows older than 90 days are deleted automatically.

Profile signals

Counts and averages that nudge estimates: how many pages, how long on them, how far you scrolled, which page categories, and whether URLs looked like product, comparison, cart or pricing pages. These are aggregates, not a history. Loki does not store the pages you visited.

Settings and diagnostics

Your mode, whether Loki is paused, and a short technical log of installs, settings changes and errors. A detailed log, which does include the sites where ads were measured, the bids seen and how each was priced, is off by default; you can switch it on in the popup to troubleshoot, and switching it off erases it. "Export diagnostics" turns the log into a file in your downloads folder, which goes nowhere unless you send it to someone.

What leaves your device

Nothing. Loki has no server in this version. The filter lists it blocks with are packaged inside the extension and never fetched, and the extension makes no network requests of its own. You can confirm that with your browser's network tools.

If a later version offers a way to contribute anonymous statistics, it will be off unless you switch it on, and this page will describe exactly what it sends before that version ships.

What Loki never collects

  • Browsing history - no record of the pages you visit leaves the device, and no list of them is kept on it either
  • Personal information - no names, emails, addresses, or account of any kind
  • Page content, searches, or form data - in Monetize me, Loki reads page text only to pick a category on the device, and never stores or sends it; in Hide me it reads nothing
  • Cookies or fingerprints - Loki sets none and reads none
  • IP addresses - Loki contacts no server, so there is nothing that could log one

Why Loki asks for access to all sites

Blocking ads and observing auctions both require running on whatever page you open, so the extension requests access to all sites. In Firefox this permission is granted by you after install. Loki uses it for the two jobs above and nothing else. The Firefox add-on declares that it collects no data.

The website

useloki.app is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and has no forms. The bids and prices that appear on the home page are a simulation that runs in your browser; nothing you do on the page is recorded or sent anywhere.

Third parties

  • GitHub Pages - hosts this website, and like any web host receives your IP address when you load a page
  • Google Fonts - serves the website's typefaces, and receives your IP address when a page loads them
  • EasyList and EasyPrivacy - the public filter lists Loki ships with; they are bundled into the extension at build time and never contacted at runtime

The extension itself involves no third party. We do not sell, rent, or share any data, and there is no personal data to share.

Your control

  • Switch modes or pause - from the popup; a page keeps the mode it loaded with until you reload it
  • Export - "Export diagnostics" gives you everything Loki holds, as a file
  • Erase - "Reset ledger" clears the ledger, profile and log and keeps your settings; uninstalling removes everything

Children's privacy

Loki is not intended for children under 13. Since Loki collects no personal information, there is nothing for us to delete on request, but you are welcome to contact us at hello@useloki.app with any concern.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what leaves your device, the extension will tell you in the popup before anything new is sent, and the date at the top of this page will change.

Contact

  • Email: hello@useloki.app
  • Website: https://useloki.app

This privacy policy is effective as of November 12, 2026, and applies to the Loki browser extension for Chrome and Firefox and to useloki.app.

LOKI IS A BROWSER EXTENSION.

It does one of two things, your choice.

HIDE ME refuses the ads and the trackers before they load. The lists the big blockers use, applied by the browser itself. No tricks, no padded counter.

MONETIZE ME leaves the ads alone and sits in the room while the auction happens. It writes down every bid, who won, whether a creative actually rendered, whether anyone bought the slot at all, and whether you looked at it.

That record is a ledger on your device. The one on this page is a toy. Yours will be real.

TERMS OF THE AUCTION

is this an ad blocker?
in Hide me, yes, a normal one. in Monetize me it is the opposite: ads stay so there is an auction to watch.
do i get paid?
no. Loki shows you what advertisers paid for your attention. it does not collect it for you. knowing the number is the point.
why is one site observed and another estimated?
Loki can only read an auction that runs in the open on the page. sites that sell ads privately show no bids, so those impressions are priced from published rates and labelled as estimates.
why are the numbers so small?
because they are. an impression trades for a fraction of a cent. the ledger adds them up across days and sites, and shows who values you most.
firefox shows a dash for blocked requests.
firefox does not tell extensions how many rules matched. rather than invent a number, Loki shows nothing. blocking works the same.
how do i know nothing is sent anywhere?
this release has no server to send to. open your browser's network tools with Loki running and look: it makes no requests of its own. the filter lists are packaged in, not fetched.
